## 1. Executive Summary & Creative Brief

### Core Premise
Derived entirely from Syracuse University’s primary messaging ecosystem, this video concept addresses the prospective student’s central aspiration: **"Who will I become?"** Rather than treating higher education as transactional course units, the narrative positions Syracuse University as a transformative ecosystem where students seize real-world capability, global opportunity, and relentless school spirit.

> [!IMPORTANT]
> **Primary Campaign Slogan:** *"It's all yours."*  
> **Sub-Positioning Statement:** *"Top-ranked academics. Transformative experiences. Lifelong connections. Here, an education amounts to more than just what you get; it is who you become."*
